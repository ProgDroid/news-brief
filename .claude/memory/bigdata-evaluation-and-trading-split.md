Risk flagged: real capital on a non-self-improving, thinly-tested system — the `validation.py` / go-live gate is the guardrail; better data feeds it but doesn't replace clearing it.

**bigdata.com = RavenPack's AI finance platform.** 1B+ docs (116M premium from ~13k publishers), real-time + 20yr archive, entity-resolved **sentiment scores** + **event detection**, earnings transcripts, filings. Two doors: (a) claude.ai **MCP connector** — free to *enable* (one-click from Claude's connector directory) but needs a bigdata account: **7-day free trial, business email required**; queries hit the same metered backend as the API, and a paid subscription is required after the trial (NOT a free-forever eval path). Interactive only — NOT usable from the headless cron/daemon pipeline; (b) **REST Search API** — pay-as-you-go, NO free tier: Fast $0.015/query, Smart $0.03, **Batch $0.0075**, Research Agent token-billed ($3/$9 per 1M + $30/1k searches — AVOID, it duplicates the Sonnet step). Docs: docs.bigdata.com.

**Real current cost (anchored 2026-06-19, Jun 1–19 usage):** 593,913 in + 22,567 out tokens + 29 web searches → **~$1.35 for 19 days ≈ $2.10/mo** at Sonnet 4.6 Batch rates ($1.50/$7.50 per M; web search $0.01/req). ~$0.07/brief. NB output is only ~1.2k tok/brief (not the 16k budget) and ~1.5 searches/brief — the brief is cheap because it leans on fed-in RSS+Chroma context, not live search. So the Anthropic spend is effectively free; **bigdata would be ~the entire cost of any new setup** (Batch 10 q/brief ≈ $2.25/mo, 30 q/brief ≈ $6.75/mo, Smart 30 q/brief ≈ $27/mo). Decision = "what is bigdata worth," not "can I afford the API." My pre-anchor estimate ($6–12/mo) was 3–5× too high.

**Trial test run (2026-06-19, MCP wired into Claude Code, n=2 symbols vs real signals-log):**
- **Sentiment tearsheet resolves the model's daily flip-flop.** CVX signals waffled bullish(6/17)→neutral(6/18)→bearish(6/19); Bigdata.com sentiment = −0.068, z −0.9(1mo)/−1.4(1qt), regime **Neutral** → adjudicates: 6/18 right, the other two overshot. Numeric+baseline+z = the backtestable feature the prose pipeline can't make.
- **Caught a blind spot:** CVX signals were 100% Hormuz macro, but entity sentiment was driven by company-specific items the pipeline missed — Tengiz accident (−0.73), $360M legal charge (−0.56), ENEOS APAC divestment (+0.74), HELLENiQ partnership (+0.83), analyst PT raises. This is the "offload research time" win made concrete.
- **Event detection:** MU events calendar flagged **Q3 earnings 24 Jun** — an active MU thesis was being held into an earnings print the pipeline didn't know about. Most actionable single finding.
- **Search** (1.2 query units, smart auto-split into 4) pulled Chevron CFO/CEO transcripts, SEC filings, dated analyst notes — beyond RSS reach — and independently corroborated the BCA $90–100 oil-floor thesis. Tearsheet even self-flags "reduced confidence" when evidence is thin.
- **Limit confirmed:** finance-centric. Nailed CVX/MU; geopolitical core (Iran MOU, Ukraine, BCA dashboard) only seen via market-impact lens → Chroma+BCA+curated RSS stays essential. Hybrid thesis holds.
- **Cost lever = fan-out:** ~1.2 units/search + separate tearsheet/events calls; a 15–20 name watchlist is where the budget gets designed.
- **Verdict:** cleared the bar on every goal (signal grounding, research offload, catalysts, quality reading) except the geopolitical core, which was never its job. Real go/no-go wants full-watchlist + archive backtest.
